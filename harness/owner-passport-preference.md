## Person analysis and visual passports

Default preference: when analyzing a new person's creative identity or enriching a visual passport, use the `person-passport` skill. Deliver varied separate clean images, descriptions and use contexts, deep source-linked spoken/written phrases, and explicit publication timing, recency and recurrence across documented occasions. Story screenshots are evidence, not final object assets. Follow the new person's own evidence; do not copy another person's identity. Text-only compilation is an intermediate step, not the completed requested passport.
