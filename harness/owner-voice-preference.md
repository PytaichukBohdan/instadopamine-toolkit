## Voice cloning and generation workflow

Default preference: use the approved InstaDopamine voice-cloning, prosody and postprocessing method by default for future requests to clone a person's voice, generate speech in it, or clean up an approved synthetic take, across projects.

Read the `voice-clone-dna` skill at `~/.agents/skills/voice-clone-dna/SKILL.md` (also installed under `~/.codex/skills/voice-clone-dna/SKILL.md`). Start from attributed, appropriate-register references and a phrase-level prosody map; prefer the approved ElevenLabs cloned-voice route when available; review Ukrainian stresses per occurrence; synthesize a coherent take; then use gentle EQ/de-essing and loudness-matched comparisons. Preserve an owner-approved take instead of automatically regenerating it. Do not default to aggressive neural cleaning, global pitch/time changes or extra limiting.

Reuse the method, not a previous person's identity, catchphrases, exact EQ or voice profile. Keep credentials, source recordings and reusable profiles private. Explicit later instructions and the new speaker's evidence take precedence over case defaults. This preference does not authorize unrelated purchases, public disclosure or verification bypasses.
