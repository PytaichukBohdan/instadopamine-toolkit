# Person passport

The owner approved the rich InstaDopamine passport on 2 October 2026 and asked to use this approach for future people. Apply the method to the new person's own evidence. The previous subject's possessions, phrases, language and appearance are not defaults.

## Collect and separate evidence

Recover feed/carousels/videos and story Highlights when accessible. If the owner supplies a browser session, use Browser Use with that session; keep cookies and signed media URLs private. Record attempted, recovered, sampled, transcribed and actually audio-reviewed coverage separately. A downloaded video is not an analyzed video. Preserve originals and hashes privately, and publish only selected, reviewed derivatives to the authorized audience.

Separate creator speech, guests, quoted dialogue, songs, written overlays, captions and editorial interpretation. Attribute people from text and source context, never by face or voice matching. Don't infer ownership, residence or private relationships from visual coappearance.

## Build reusable assets, not screenshot cards

Make a varied inventory: wardrobe, eyewear, accessories, plaques, cameras, bottles, instruments, animals, furniture, rooms, scenes, gestures and meaningful written phrases when supported. Include ordinary recurring details as well as striking one-offs. Depth means more distinct evidence-backed assets, not duplicating near-identical poses or inventing a quota.

Each item needs a **separate clean image + description + context for use**, a practical scene brief, limits, source links and exact source locators. Keep story overlays and UI out of the asset image; preserve original screenshots in a separate evidence atlas. Use the available imagegen skill for clean reconstructions; mark every reconstruction as AI interpretation, never proof or an exact product replica. Keep separate thumbnails and full downloads. Retain prompts, model and output/input hashes.

## Treat time as two independent axes

For visuals, written phrases, spoken phrases and identity claims, record publication time with provenance, retrieval time, and video offset separately. Record filming/event time only if independently established. Keep unknown dates unknown.

Show first/last observed publication, analysis date, age of latest known evidence, distinct source IDs, publication days and **documented occasions**. Deduplicate reposts, repeated frames, cross-listed Highlights and alternate crops. Group a run of stories from one trip/shoot as one occasion. Different publication days alone do not prove independent events.

Keep recency separate from repetition: a recent one-off, an old one-off, a historical recurring motif and a recently repeated pattern are different. Use explicit display windows (project default: recent ≤180 days, older 181–730, historical >730); these are navigation aids, not scientific thresholds or proof of current relevance. Unknown event groupings stay unknown. Never equate archive counts with how often something happens in real life. Say “observed in N sources / M documented occasions,” not “always” or “daily.”

In scene suggestions, prioritize recently supported recurring motifs where available; retain old one-offs as dated optional references. Do not silently combine incompatible historical looks into a supposedly current outfit. If only historical evidence exists, say so and don't fabricate an update.

## Deepen speech and identity

Build a broad, useful phrase bank from actual audio: audience address, pivots, questions, explanation, self-correction, understatement, humor, invitations and exits. Use the voice-clone-dna skill when available for performance analysis or synthesis. For each short example preserve original words/language, approximate audio window, date, attribution basis, delivery, uncertainty and context for reuse. Automated listening is not human verification.

Count exact wording separately from phrase families or rhetorical functions. Translations and newly authored lines are adaptations; written slogans have unverified pronunciation until actual audio is found. Keep guest lines and music out of the creator's voice bank. Source audio excerpts stay private or on an explicitly authorized protected listening surface, never silently in a public build. Synthesized audio has its own origin label.

Identity conclusions should describe supported creative patterns across sources, dates and contexts. One video's views, revenue, prizes or follower count are time-bound claims, not personal DNA. Preserve counterexamples, language/register changes and historical limits. Do not clone or regenerate speech solely because a phrase bank was requested.

## Finish the workflow

Text synthesis is an intermediate research stage. A full requested passport is not complete until the separate assets, descriptions, contexts, dated evidence, useful phrase bank, coverage and browsable report are delivered, or specific access/generation blockers are stated. In InstaDopamine use the repository's manifests and validation/build pipeline; do not hard-code the previous person's name or private paths into a new analysis.

Verify original links/locators, dates and deduplication, generated-asset hashes, mobile browsing, downloads and actual-host audio seeking. Keep private data protected on every production, preview and direct deployment URL. Current user authorization and delivery preferences govern publishing; this skill does not grant new external permissions.
